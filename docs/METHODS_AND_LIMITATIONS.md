# Methods updates

The source snapshot, original sample, seed, review weights and submitted judgments are unchanged. The primary RQ2 estimand changed from a redress rate among non-unclear records to documented-redress coverage across all reviewed cases. In the construction cohort, 19 cases are coded documented and 111 unclear, with none explicitly absent. Dropping unclear cases would leave only positive outcomes. That contrast is therefore suppressed, rather than reported as evidence of equal remedy access.

The reviewed evidence map and scenario results represent the supplied labels and judgments. They require source-level appraisal before use in a journal manuscript. All current evidence links point to AIID incident pages; specific underlying report citations and field-specific supporting evidence would strengthen reproducibility. AI assistance must remain disclosed. Scenarios are conditional narratives, not predictions or probabilities. Thresholds are illustrative; the new sensitivity grid tests clarity thresholds 0.60/0.70/0.80 and redress-documentation thresholds 0.15/0.25/0.35.

Proposed manuscripts should distinguish a documented complaint or appeal route from a successful remedy, and unknown documentation from absence. Accountability clarity may be coded clear even when the exact developer/deployer relationship remains unknown; explain that distinction. Low/high scenario axis states describe stipulated conditions, rather than verified causal properties of the archive. Retain weak and contradicting evidence, particularly the small basis for S3. Later-case selection and any thresholds chosen after seeing results must be disclosed as exploratory.
